Account & Data Deletion

Request permanent erasure of your account, photos, and facial data

Google Play User Data Compliance: In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy, Fast Send provides a clear, accessible mechanism for all users (both registered organizers and event guests) to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

How to Delete Your Data

You can delete your data through either of the two methods below:

Method 1: In-App (For Organizers)

Delete Directly Inside the Android App

If you have the Fast Send mobile application installed, you can immediately delete your account and all associated trips at any time:

  1. Open the Fast Send app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab on the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account Settings section.
  4. Tap Delete Account & All Data.
  5. Confirm your password. Your account, trips, photos, and facial recognition vectors will be immediately and irreversibly purged from our servers.
Method 2: Web Request (For Guests & Organizers)

Submit a Web Deletion Request

If you are an event guest who accessed the web portal, or an organizer who no longer has the app installed, submit this request form. Our systems will process your request within 48 hours.

Request Received! Your data deletion ticket has been registered. You will receive a confirmation once all photos, selfies, and biometric embedding records have been permanently expunged from our database and cloud storage.

What Data Is Deleted?

When an account or guest record deletion is processed, our system permanently purges:

  • Biometric Embeddings: All 512-dimensional facial recognition vectors associated with your face.
  • Selfie Reference Images: The reference selfie photo you captured during registration.
  • Uploaded Event Photos: If you are an organizer deleting a trip or account, all original high-resolution assets stored in Microsoft Azure Blob Storage are permanently destroyed.
  • Profile & Contact Records: Your email address, Firebase authentication UID, name, phone number, and sync tokens.
  • Notification Records: All push notifications and delivery logs.

Data Retention Policy

Fast Send does not retain user data indefinitely:

  • Guest Selfies & Vectors: Stored only while the corresponding trip is active. When the organizer archives or deletes the trip, all attendee vectors for that trip are automatically purged.
  • Cloud Backups: System backups containing residual deleted records are overwritten and permanently destroyed within 30 days.
